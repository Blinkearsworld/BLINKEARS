The Blinkears

One family.

They love each other. They annoy each other. They usually make things worse together.

01 Dad

Rumba

The easygoing dad

Rumba keeps the family grounded with his relaxed attitude, clever ideas and a great sense of humor — and he’s always ready to fix a problem, including the ones he accidentally creates.

  • Funny

The warm and creative mom

Samba is the heart of the family: warm, creative and usually three steps ahead. She can see the disaster coming. Stopping it is another matter.

  • Creative

Siko

Full of questions, ideas and energy. Siko explores, experiments and is convinced every idea deserves a test run. Preferably immediately.
