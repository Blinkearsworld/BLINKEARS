Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

This website is the official home of BLINKEARS. It is a static website. We do not have user accounts, we do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not set cookies ourselves.

For parents

  • No sign-up, no ads, no in-game purchases. All games and cards are free and work without creating an account.
  • Nothing is sent to us. Game progress, best scores and the name typed into a game stay only on your own device (in the browser’s local storage). You can delete them at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.
  • Share links contain what you type. When you send a “Challenge a friend” link or a Birthday Card, the name (and, for cards, the age and “from” name) you typed is written into the link itself so the card can show it. It is not stored by us. We suggest using a first name or a nickname.
  • Sharing is always your choice. Nothing is posted anywhere automatically. Sharing opens your device’s own share menu or copies a link.
  • Videos come from YouTube. See “Third-party services” below.

Email

If you contact us by email (for example, to ask about merch or to be notified at launch), we use your email address only to reply to you and to send the updates you asked for. We do not sell or share it. Children should ask a parent before emailing us.

Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and the video list.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts.
  • YouTube provides video thumbnails and the videos on the Watch page and home page. Videos use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). When you press play, YouTube may store information on your device and process data under its own privacy policy.

These providers may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver their content. See their own privacy policies for details.

Changes

If analytics, newsletters, online shopping or other services are added later, this policy will be updated before those features are activated.
